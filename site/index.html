PREDICTIVE

Beliefs updated by games.

Predictive combines preseason beliefs with eligible game results to estimate current team quality. Context starts from history plus offseason roster and recruiting information; History starts from prior program performance only.

PERFORMANCE

What the games say.

Performance removes the team's own preseason prior and asks what quality is implied by the football it has played. Predictive Context estimates still help interpret opponent strength.

Performance is not strength of record, standings, or postseason deservingness. It begins after eligible games have been played; teams without one are shown as NR.
