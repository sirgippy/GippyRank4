About these rankings

Predictive

GippyRank models team quality as a probability distribution over rank-like states. Predictive Context and Predictive History are alternative preseason priors that the same game-evidence engine updates.

Performance

Performance asks who has played best so far, using completed games and opponent beliefs. It is separate from Predictive and is not standings, strength of record, or postseason deservingness.
